# Snack Shack

A snack table run by parent volunteers after lunch at a BC elementary school, raising money for the PAC. The app tracks stock, checks the cash after each sale, and handles buying and reimbursements. Words here were chosen with the volunteers; avoid retail jargon.

## Language

### People

**Volunteer**:
A parent on the team who runs sale days, buys stock and edits items, each on their own phone and login.
_Avoid_: User, staff, cashier

**Coordinator**:
The volunteer who also manages the team list and settings (the admin role).
_Avoid_: Manager, owner

**Treasurer**:
The PAC treasurer who reimburses claims from a weekly email and never opens the app.
_Avoid_: Approver, accountant

**Student helper**:
A student who helps at the table on a sale day and earns a helper credit.
_Avoid_: Kid volunteer

**Kid**:
A student (aged 5–12) buying at the table.
_Avoid_: Customer, shopper

### Sale day

**Sale day**:
One after-lunch selling period, from picking the lineup to the finished count up.
_Avoid_: Session, shift, recess sale

**Lineup**:
The 4–6 items put out, as whole boxes, for one sale day. Everything else stays in storage untouched.
_Avoid_: Menu, selection

**Check stock**:
Counting each lineup box before selling and recording anything **missing**, **damaged** or **found**.
_Avoid_: Pre-count, opening count

**Start sale**:
The moment check stock ends and selling begins; each lineup item's price, deal size and type lock for the day.

**Count up**:
After selling: count what's left of each lineup item, count the cash, then sign off.
_Avoid_: Close-out, reconciliation, end of day

**Sign-off**:
A volunteer confirming the count up.
_Avoid_: Approval, initials

**Out**:
Pieces removed during a sale without being sold (melted, dropped). Not the same as missing or damaged at check stock.
_Avoid_: Waste, shrinkage, loss

**Change float**:
The fixed cash kept in the box for making change (normally $30).
_Avoid_: Float, till, starting cash

**Helper credit**:
$1 of snacks given to the student helper with no cash taken; lowers expected cash by $1.
_Avoid_: Freebie, comp

**Expected cash**:
Change float plus sales minus helper credits.

**Over / short**:
Cash counted minus expected cash.
_Avoid_: Variance, discrepancy

**Deposit**:
Cash counted minus the change float; sealed in the deposit bag for the PAC.

### Items and prices

**Item**:
One thing the kids can buy, counted in pieces. An assorted variety pack is one item.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU

**Piece**:
The unit stock is counted in (one bar, one bag, one popsicle), even for deal items.
_Avoid_: Unit, each

**Type**:
**Snack** or **Treat**. The only split the kid rules use.
_Avoid_: Category

**Storage**:
**Shelf** or **Freezer**; a label telling volunteers where to fetch a box. It never affects rules or grouping.
_Avoid_: Location, type

**Kid rules**:
Each kid buys at most 3 items, of which at most 1 is a treat.
_Avoid_: Basket limit

**Price option**:
One of the allowed prices: $1, $2, 2 for $1, 3 for $1.

**Deal**:
A multi-piece price option such as "2 for $1". Counts as one item (and one treat, if a treat) for the kid rules.
_Avoid_: Bundle, combo, multi-buy

**Suggested price**:
The price option the app recommends from an item's cost per piece.

**Needs a price**:
A new item logged with "Decide later"; it can't go in a lineup until priced.

**Cost per piece**:
What one piece cost the team, tax included.
_Avoid_: Unit cost, COGS

**Margin**:
The share of a price option that is profit: 1 − (cost per piece × pieces per deal) ÷ price.
_Avoid_: Markup

**Margin band**:
The label for a margin: **Great deal**, **Good**, **Fair**, **Low margin**, **At a loss**.

### Stock and buying

**Stock**:
Pieces on hand, the sum of an item's stock movements.
_Avoid_: Inventory

**Stock movement**:
One recorded change to an item's stock: purchase, sold, out, missing, damaged, found, donated or correction.
_Avoid_: Adjustment, transaction

**Target stock**:
About 2 sale days' worth, the most storage holds.
_Avoid_: Par, par level, reorder point

**Shopping trip**:
A volunteer's claim on the next buying trip ("I'm going shopping") so two people don't buy the same things.

**Deal check**:
The in-aisle calculator for judging whether a box is worth buying.
_Avoid_: Price check

**What to buy**:
Guidance on stock gaps by type, not a shopping list.
_Avoid_: Shopping list, reorder list

**Purchase**:
One receipt's worth of snack lines, logged once with a required receipt photo.
_Avoid_: Order

**Claim**:
A logged purchase waiting to be reimbursed, numbered SS-001, SS-002, …; either **To pay** or **Paid**.
_Avoid_: Expense, invoice, reimbursement request
