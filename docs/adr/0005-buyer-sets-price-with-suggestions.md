# The buyer sets the price, guided by suggestions and margin bands

Prices are limited to $1, $2, 2 for $1 and 3 for $1, whole-dollar cash prices kids understand; deals make very cheap items (mini bars at 13.8¢) sensible. The buyer picks the price when logging a purchase, helped by a suggested price and five margin bands (Great deal ≥60%, Good 45–60%, Fair 30–45%, Low margin 0–30%, At a loss <0%). "Low margin" states a fact without saying don't buy, and "At a loss" always shows the amount lost. The lineup shows its overall margin because a low-margin item is fine when the table as a whole makes money. New items can wait for a price ("Decide later") but can't go in a lineup until priced. No voting or approval: it's the lowest-friction option for a casual team.
