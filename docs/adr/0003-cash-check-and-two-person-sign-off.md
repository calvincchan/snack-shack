# Cash check: fixed float, tolerances, helper credit, two-person sign-off

The change float is fixed (default $30) so volunteers always know what to leave in the box. Over/short within ±$3 is ok and within ±$10 is a warning (recount once, add a note); small differences are normal with kids. The student helper's $1 snack credit is a no-cash deduction from expected cash, not a loss or a shortage. Every count up needs **two different signed-in volunteers** to sign off, enforced by the database; this protects volunteers as much as the money. That is only meaningful because each volunteer signs in on their own phone with an email magic link that lasts all term, which also powers the audit trail and presence.
