# What to buy is guidance, not a shopping list

Buyers choose by availability, recent sales and new finds, so What to buy shows stock gaps by type against a target of about 2 sale days (what storage holds; trips are irregular), not a list of products. A Deal check calculator (GST toggle on by default, since snack foods usually carry GST) helps judge a box in the aisle. A shopping trip claim ("I'm going shopping") stops two people buying the same things; any free volunteer can shop, and showing the claim is enough (no locking).

## Considered options

- **Par-level shopping list with fixed products**: rejected; doesn't match how buyers actually shop.
