# Count stock instead of recording sales

The line of kids is too fast for anyone to tap an app per sale, so the app never records individual sales. Volunteers pick a lineup of 4–6 items, bring out **whole boxes**, check stock before selling, and count what's left afterwards. Sales, stock and the cash check all follow from those two counts. Losses found at check stock (missing, damaged, found) are fixed before any money changes hands, so over/short measures only the cash. Only lineup items are counted; storage isn't touched on a sale day.

## Considered options

- **Recording every transaction at the till:** rejected; too slow for a line of kids.
- **Bringing out part of a box:** rejected; it needs a "brought out" and a "returned" count and is harder to understand than whole boxes.
